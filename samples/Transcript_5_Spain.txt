Expert 5 – Dr. Elena Gomez
Role: Chief of Surgical Services & Urology Specialist
Market: Spain

00:00
Interviewer: Welcome, Dr. Gomez. To start, how would you evaluate robotic surgery adoption in Spain today?

00:15
Dr. Gomez: Adoption in Spain has accelerated over the past few years, particularly in major autonomous communities like Madrid and Catalonia. However, access remains fragmented across smaller regional public hospitals.

01:02
Interviewer: What do you see as the main barriers to wider adoption?

01:08
Dr. Gomez: High initial acquisition cost and ongoing consumable expenses are the main obstacles. In addition, allocating sufficient theatre time and managing surgeon learning curves without impacting patient throughput pose operational challenges.

02:00
Interviewer: How important are hospital financial budgets and ROI in Spain's purchasing decisions?

02:06
Dr. Gomez: ROI is very important, though public health authorities also weigh clinical outcome parameters such as reduced complication rates and shorter hospital stays. However, without a convincing financial utilization model, approval is rarely granted.

03:00
Interviewer: What role does surgeon training play in hospital purchasing choices?

03:05
Dr. Gomez: Training is paramount. Spanish hospitals prefer vendors offering comprehensive certification and simulation programs. Broad training across urology, gynecology, and general surgery ensures the system achieves high weekly case volumes.

04:00
Interviewer: What trend do you foresee over the next 3 to 5 years?

04:06
Dr. Gomez: We expect procedure growth of 12 to 15 percent annually. Increased competition among robotic vendors should drive down system costs, enabling secondary hospitals to adopt the technology.

05:00
Interviewer: What is the average hospital purchasing timeline in Spain?

05:05
Dr. Gomez: Public hospital tenders generally require 9 to 15 months due to administrative regulations and regional budget planning cycles. Private hospital networks can decide faster, usually within 6 to 8 months.
